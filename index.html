Montserrat Pagan

Hello, I’m Montserrat! I'm currently a staff writer for AIM Media Texas in the Rio Grande Valley. My coverage mainly focuses on local government, courts and crime in Texas-Mexico border cities such as Brownsville (the largest in the Rio Grande Valley) and Starbase (the newest Texas city with a population of majority SpaceX employees). I recently graduated with a Bachelor of Arts from New York University. I studied Journalism and Comparative Literature with a minor in Data Science. I love narrative non-fiction, data storytelling, and creative multimedia photo/video production.
