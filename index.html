Montserrat Pagan

Hello, I’m Montserrat!

I'm currently a staff writer for AIM Media Texas in the Rio Grande Valley. My coverage mainly focuses on local government, courts, and crime in Texas-Mexico border cities such as Brownsville (the largest in the Rio Grande Valley) and Starbase (the newest Texas city, founded by SpaceX employees). My work is published online at myRGV.com and printed in The Brownsville Herald, Harlingen's Valley Morning Star, and The Monitor in McAllen.

I have Bachelor of Arts degree from New York University, where I studied Journalism and Comparative Literature with a minor in Data Science. I love narrative non-fiction, data storytelling, and creative multimedia photo/video production.
